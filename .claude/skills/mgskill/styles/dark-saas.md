# Style: Dark SaaS (example)

An example of transferring the same knowledge to a dark tech look (the "UI inspiration" board: glowing blue/purple dashboards). Swap freely.

## Colours (3 tones)
- Ground: near-black navy radial gradient #0B0F1A → #05070D, faint 1 px grid (white 4%), a soft accent glow behind the focal point, fine noise
- Ink: #EDEFF5 · muted #7D8597
- Accent: #6C7BFF (indigo), glow rgba(108,123,255,0.5)
- Captions on footage: #F3F3F3

## Typography
- Sans: Inter 500/600
- Display: Instrument Serif Italic, or a condensed bold (Inter Tight 800) for numbers
- Sizes: 36 / 76 px

## Materials
- Cards: glass, rgba(255,255,255,0.06) fill, 1 px rgba(255,255,255,0.12) border, a 20 px blur backdrop, an inner top highlight, an accent rim-light on the focal card
- Charts: thin 3 px accent lines with a glow, a soft area gradient under them
- UI over footage: the same glass, darker (rgba(10,12,20,.75))

## Motion
- Energy: medium-high. Square reveals, light sweeps across cards on entrance, numbers count up, the camera slowly orbits (small rotate ±2°)
