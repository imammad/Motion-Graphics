# Style: Warm Editorial (example)

The "yellow + casted shadow + grid" look from the visual guide: magazine/poster feel, halftone objects.

## Colours (3 tones)
- Ground: yellow-to-greyish gradient #EFE6C8 → #D9D4C7, casted window shadow, sometimes a thin grey grid
- Ink: #1A1A1A · muted #6F6A5E
- Accent: #D9482B (dulled vermilion) or a deep blue #2342C8
- Captions on footage: #F3F0E8

## Typography
- Sans: Inter 600 (tight tracking for headlines)
- Display: EB Garamond Bold Italic or Playfair Display Italic
- Sizes: 34 / 80 px

## Materials
- Objects: halftone / black-and-white cut-outs (brain, eye, chess piece, hand) with one accent shape behind them (circle, square)
- Cards: off-white paper cards with a hard-ish shadow, slightly rotated (±3°)
- UI over footage: dark glass, the same as blue-minimal

## Motion
- Energy: calm. Longer holds, slow camera drift, circle reveals, the accent shape scales in behind the object first
